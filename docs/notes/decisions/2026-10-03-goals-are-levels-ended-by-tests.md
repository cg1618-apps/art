# Goals are levels, and every level and stage ends in a test

The roadmap was agreed with the owner, and the migration seeds it
(`data-model.md`, "The roadmap seed").

- **Levels are named for what you can draw** — 基礎, 人體, 角色, 場景, 上色,
  插畫 — not short / mid / long term. At ten minutes a day nobody can promise a
  duration; a level is passed by its test, never by a date. Splitting "short
  term" into the figure and the finished character, and adding a foundations
  level below it, is what makes six.
- **Every level and stage carries a test**: a piece redrawn over time, which
  gives progress a picture rather than only hours. Test records attach to it.
- **The order follows what the owner values**: perspective and proportion
  before detail, "rough but good" over "it looks weird". The figure in
  perspective is the stage that fixes "it looks weird"; muscle anatomy comes
  after gesture and stays at the level of masses; composition waits for the
  scene level, since a lone character needs little of it. Line drills are a
  warm-up rather than a stage of months.
- **Status is set by hand**, not derived from the stages, so a level can be
  passed on its test before every stage is ticked.
- **What is being worked on is the status, never the position.** The owner
  does not go top to bottom or one stage at a time, so the roadmap highlights
  every stage and level set to 進行中, however many and wherever they are.
  Rejected: one derived "current stage", the first not passed.
- **The stage number is derived** from the order, so reordering never leaves a
  stale number behind.
- **A goal with stages is RESTRICT, not CASCADE**: the stages are the roadmap,
  and deleting a level header is the wrong way to lose them.
- **Goals and Roadmap are one module in practice**: a level is nothing without
  its stages, and neither page is useful alone.
