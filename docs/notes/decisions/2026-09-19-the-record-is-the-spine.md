# The record is the spine, and the timer is optional

The stopwatch is one way to produce a record, never the only way: duration is
nullable, a record can be written after the fact, and the app is fully usable
by someone who never starts a timer. Nothing demands a drill or a stage before
a record can be written either.

Rejected: sessions created only by the recorder. That would make the app
useless on every day the recorder was not used, which is most of them.

**The principle is platform-wide**, and this is the third model it decided,
after `food`'s ingredient stubs and `travel`'s trip-less packing lists: **no
app may demand bookkeeping before it is useful.** Anything that requires a
parent record, a timer or a setup step before the thing you actually want to
do is a reason the app stops being opened.
