# Expressions and accessories are groups on references, not libraries

The owner asked for libraries of expressions and accessories. They are the
same kind of thing as any other reference, so they are groups — 表情 and 配件
are `reference_group` options — rather than tables of their own. A library is
one option value.

Rejected: a table per library, which would mean near-identical screens to
build and maintain for each one.
